FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Need help? Find answers here.

Quick information about the catalogue and enquiry process.

Contact our team →
What product categories are listed?

The catalogue includes PLCs, AC drives, servo products, HMI, SCADA and IPC, DCS, control panels, PCC/MCC panels and switchgear.

Can I ask for a quote using a specific model number?

Yes. Enter the product or model in the enquiry form. You can also include a part number, quantity and delivery location.

Does the catalogue show current stock or pricing?

No. Contact the team to confirm product specifications, availability, pricing and delivery details.

How do I send the enquiry?

Choose WhatsApp or email. Your contact app opens with a draft message; review it and press Send.
